How your material is handled

  • Nothing you upload is modified. The tool reads your documents and writes a report. Your files stay as you uploaded them, with every version kept.
  • Verdicts are deterministic. Whether a rule passes or fails is decided by the rule itself over the facts found in your document, not by a model's opinion. The model explains findings and drafts suggestions; it never decides them.
  • Every finding carries its evidence. Each result points at the passage that produced it, so you can check the tool rather than trust it.
